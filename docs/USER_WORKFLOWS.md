# Road Warrior User Workflows

This document will describe the user journeys and workflows that Road Warrior must support once they have been researched and approved.

## Users

- Bruce is the primary user.

## Primary Workflows

### Driving Conversation

1. Before driving, Bruce may manually open and start Road Warrior.
2. Once driving begins, interaction with Road Warrior should be hands-free.
3. Android Auto navigation remains visible and usable while the Road Warrior voice session runs concurrently.
4. Voice input and Road Warrior replies use the car audio system where supported.
5. Bruce begins with whatever portion of a task, project-related idea, or possible new-project idea is currently formed.
6. Road Warrior maintains the conversational context and helps Bruce develop the thought.
7. The conversation produces brainstorming results, action items, or both.
8. After the session, Road Warrior preserves the discussion, decisions, and action items for later use.
9. The exact end-of-conversation confirmation and review experience is TBD.

## Alternate Workflows
