## Experience 000 — Cognitive Relief

**Priority:** TBD (P0/P1/P2)

### Purpose

Remove unnecessary cognitive burden by safely carrying small thoughts, reminders, questions, and commitments.

### Lifecycle

- **Capture:** Receive the thought, reminder, question, or commitment without disrupting momentum.
- **Carry:** Hold the captured item so the user no longer needs to carry it mentally.
- **Recognize Context:** Notice when the surrounding context makes the item useful.
- **Reopen Collaboration:** Bring the item back as an invitation to continue, clarify, or act.
- **Release:** Stop carrying the item when it has been resolved or is no longer needed.

### Typical Conversations

- "Remind me to call Ben tomorrow."
- "Capture that."
- "What's my first appointment Tuesday?"
- "Don't let me forget dog food."
- "Put this in Horizon."
- Coffee before beginning a drive.
- Bank before beginning a drive.
- Dim Sum when arriving to visit Bob in Los Angeles.
- Resume Road Warrior discussion.
- Resume a previous project discussion.
- Bring something back during the next conversation with Ben.

### Contextual Opportunities

Many captured items are opportunities rather than conventional reminders.

Road Warrior should return them when the surrounding human context makes them valuable.

If several reasonable continuations exist, Road Warrior should reopen the conversation instead of assuming the desired action.

### Road Warrior Behavior

- Understand intent naturally.
- Remove the burden immediately.
- Confirm briefly.
- Never overcomplicate the interaction.

### Trust Test

Does the user immediately stop carrying the thought mentally?

## Experience 001 — Continuity

**Priority:** TBD (P0/P1/P2)

### Purpose

Allow conversations to continue naturally across time without reconstructing context.

### Typical Conversations

#### Pattern A — User Recall

- "Do you remember..."
- "Let's continue..."
- "Where were we..."

#### Pattern B — Deferred Conversation

- "Remind me next time we're driving..."
- "Bring this back tomorrow..."
- "Let's continue this next week..."

### Road Warrior Behavior

- Restore context quickly.
- Resume naturally.
- Never require document hunting or session management.

### Trust Test
