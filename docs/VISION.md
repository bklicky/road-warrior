# Road Warrior Vision

- Version: 0.1
- Date: 2026-08-01

This document provides a concise entry point to Road Warrior's vision and intended outcomes. The [Foundation](FOUNDATION.md) is the canonical baseline.

## Vision Statement

Help people become more of who they are capable of becoming.

## Intended Outcomes

- Bruce can capture a task that must not be forgotten without stopping to decide where it belongs.
- Bruce can explore an idea connected to an existing project through an intelligent conversation.
- Bruce can explore an early idea that may become a new project.
- Half-baked concepts can be developed through discussion instead of requiring Bruce to fully structure them before capture.
- The discussion, resulting decisions, and action items are preserved for later use.
- Road Warrior can be used conversationally while Bruce is actively driving and wherever he is working at a computer.

## Boundaries

- Bruce is the primary user.
- The first version is centered on conversations that produce brainstorming results, action items, or both.
- The August 21, 2026 prototype is field research; useful evidence either way is the objective.
